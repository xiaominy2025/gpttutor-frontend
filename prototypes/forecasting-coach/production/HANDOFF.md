The runtime refuses missing or inconsistent rows and never initializes a new durable row.
- Production Navigator and Forecasting share **10,000,000 micro-USD per UTC calendar month** on the canonical Navigator budget row. Forecasting verifies and uses the same versioned CAS as Navigator.
